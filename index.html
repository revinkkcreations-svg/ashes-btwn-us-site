Behind the noise

Our
Story

Some things are too heavy to carry in silence.

Ashes Btwn Us didn't start with a plan to build a band. It started with a need to get things out.

Life has a way of leaving things behind. Memories that won't fade, words left unsaid, anger, regret, and experiences that change you in ways you don't always understand. Sometimes you need somewhere to put all of it. For Ashes Btwn Us, that place became music.

From Personal Outlet to Something More

What began as a personal creative outlet slowly grew into something bigger. Songwriting became a way to process life, confront difficult emotions, and put thoughts into words that weren't always easy to say out loud.

For roughly three years, the writing continued. Songs were developed, ideas were tested, and the sound evolved through experimentation, persistence, and plenty of learning along the way. It wasn't always a straightforward process. Life gets busy, schedules don't always line up, and creating music doesn't happen in a vacuum. But the work kept moving forward.

Eventually, a collection of personal thoughts and individual songs began to take shape as something more than a private outlet. It became Ashes Btwn Us.

The Music Between the Lines

Blending elements of emo, punk, alternative rock, and darker melodic sounds, Ashes Btwn Us brings together heavy guitars, driving bass, emotional melodies, and lyrics that don't always take the easy way out.

The songs explore memory, loss, anxiety, relationships, nostalgia, frustration, and the strange contradictions of being human. Some are serious. Some lean into dark humor. Some are loud and defiant, while others sit with feelings that are harder to explain.

Because life isn't one emotion, and neither is this music.

Ashes Btwn Us isn't here to preach, pretend to have all the answers, or tell anyone how to live. The songs come from personal experiences, observations, and opinions. They're an honest expression of a particular point of view, with room for listeners to find their own meaning in the words.

If a lyric makes somebody feel understood, or a song gives someone a place to put feelings they've been carrying around, then the music has connected in the way it was meant to.

Night Terrors

The debut release, Night Terrors (Demo), brings ten songs together into a snapshot of the project in its earliest released form. It's the result of years of writing, learning, and working toward getting the music out into the world.

The word Demo is intentional. This release captures where the songs and the project stood at that moment, while leaving room for the music to keep evolving. It's a beginning, not a finish line.

The Story Continues

Ashes Btwn Us began as a way to give difficult thoughts and complicated feelings somewhere to go. That purpose hasn't changed, even as the project has grown.

There will always be more to write about, more sounds to explore, and more stories to tell. Not every memory fades. Not every question gets answered. And not everything that hurts needs to be hidden.

Sometimes you just have to turn it up and let it out.

“This is Ashes Btwn Us. Some things are too heavy to carry in silence.”
