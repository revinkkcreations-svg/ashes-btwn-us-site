Behind the noise

Some things
never stay
buried.

Ashes Btwn Us is an alternative emo project built around honest songwriting, restless guitars, heavy bass, and the parts of life that don't neatly resolve.

Night Terrors began as a personal creative outlet and grew into a collection of songs about memory, loss, anxiety, and growing up. This is music for the things we carry, the things we can't explain, and the noise we make trying to understand them.
